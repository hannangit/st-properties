A clear, four-step move-in.

Found a room you like? Here's exactly what happens next. You only need your documents once you've enquired and reserved — not before.

1

Enquire & view

Send an enquiry on the room, then book a viewing or video tour.

2

Reserve the room

Pay a £280 reservation deposit to secure it and pause further viewings.

3

Referencing

Send your documents so we can begin referencing.

4

Move in

Sign your agreement and collect the keys.

Only once your room is confirmed — nothing is needed to start searching.

  • 💷
    Proof of income — last 3 months' payslips.
  • 🏦
    Bank statements — showing rent to your previous landlord.
  • 📄
    New job? Employer letter confirming offer, hours and contract length.
  • 🛂
    Photo ID — a valid passport.
  • Right to Rent share code — from gov.uk/prove-right-to-rent.

Missing one of these? Contact us before reserving and we'll explain your options.

Reservation Deposit
£280

Secures your room, stops us advertising it, and pauses viewings while referencing completes.

Securing your accommodation

  • To secure accommodation with ST Properties Ltd, a deposit equivalent to three weeks' rent or £280 is required.
  • The total rental charge must be paid no later than 24 hours prior to arrival, or on the day of arrival, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • A booking is not confirmed until ST Properties Ltd acknowledges receipt of either the deposit or the full payment.
Late payment:
  • Failure to adhere to the agreed payment terms may result in suspension of accommodation.
  • ST Properties Ltd reserves the right to charge interest on overdue accounts.

See the room in person or on a video call before you reserve.
